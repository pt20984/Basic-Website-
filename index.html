Introduction

Photography has been a long-time hobby and has become a favorite art-medium of mine. I began taking taking photos in highschool and have been ever since. My main focus is in capturing portraits as well as plants, nature, and animals. Taking photos of nature brings me closer and more in touch with the natural world. I find it very fascinating that I can capture different perspectives of nature in ways that the human eye can not see.
